Creation has always changed.
So has everything it hides.

Three media. Three sets. Each set asks the same question: which of these feels most like an act of creation? You will judge first, learn how each was made, and then learn what that making rests on. Your judgment will be asked again at every stage. The project does not offer a conclusion. It asks you to notice your own.

Creation has always changed.

The change you saw, heard, watched today is different from the changes before. In scale, in speed, in the conditions of labor and consent that produced what reached you.
